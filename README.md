#### Simulated Engagement
Nubinero Technologies Pvt. Ltd., it's people, and it's events are **fictional**. Any resemblance found to real companies, people, and events is a coincidence. Nothing here is a legal advice. 

### The scenario
Nubinero Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional B2B SaaS company based in Pune. It operates in automating account payables(invoice captures, approvals, vendor payments, reconciliation) and offers an embedded analytics module that connects to customer's ERP, bank feeds and warehouses. The enterprise prospects now require ISO/SAE 27001 Certification and a recent phishing incident has worried the board. The engagament assesses where Nubinero stands, identfies the gaps, and builds a roadmap towards the ISO/SAE 27001 Certification.

### Phase 1: Client Profile
This is the foundation of the document. Before any control is assessed a consultant has to understand the case, what needs to be protected, and who the client is. The reasons for the client company to apply for a certification, their security posture and the overall scope is analysed as well. <br>
**You can read it here**: [View PDF](Phase1_clientpf.pdf)
#### What it contains
- **Client**: document control, snapshot, business context, stakeholders and technology and data landscape.
- **Why and where they sit:** The engagement background, the security posture labeled as reported by the client, the regulatory and contractual drivers
- **The plan:** scope, assumptions, areas of focus and the engagement plan. 
#### What it demostrates
- Scoping an engagement, stakeholder mapping.
- Differentiating between what a client says and what is assessed later.
- Be explicit about assumptions and open questions, research regulation, record date and sources checked.
